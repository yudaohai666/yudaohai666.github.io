About简介

I am a graduate student at Xiamen University, working on efficient AI. My research focuses on large language models for long-text processing and on multimodal large models, with an emphasis on efficient long-context training and inference.

我是厦门大学的一名研究生，研究方向为高效人工智能，主要关注面向长文本处理的大语言模型与多模态大模型，尤其关注长上下文的高效训练与推理。

I am fortunate to be advised by Prof. Rongrong Ji at the MAC Lab, and co-advised by Asst. Prof. Zhanpeng Zeng.

我很荣幸师从 MAC 实验室的纪荣嵘教授，并由曾展鹏助理教授联合指导。
